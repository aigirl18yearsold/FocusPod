# 🎯 FocusPod

> **Less distraction. More achievement.**

FocusPod is a low-cost, distraction-free pocket productivity companion designed to help students stay organized without constantly unlocking their smartphones.

Unlike a smartphone, FocusPod focuses only on productivity. It displays schedules, reminders, study plans, and important tasks while reducing digital distractions.

---

# 📖 Overview

Students often unlock their phones to check their schedules or reminders, only to become distracted by social media, games, or notifications.

FocusPod provides a dedicated pocket device that synchronizes with a mobile phone via Bluetooth, allowing users to stay focused on their academic goals.

---

# 🚀 Vision

To create an affordable, portable productivity companion that helps students build better study habits while reducing unnecessary smartphone usage.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Many students:

- Check one reminder and spend 30 minutes scrolling social media.
- Miss important deadlines.
- Forget assignments.
- Lose focus during study sessions.
- Depend completely on smartphones.

Current productivity apps still require users to unlock their phones, increasing distraction.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

FocusPod is a dedicated productivity device that:

- Displays today's schedule
- Gives reminder notifications
- Plays voice reminders
- Connects to a smartphone using Bluetooth
- Allows quick interaction using physical buttons
- Keeps students focused without unnecessary apps
